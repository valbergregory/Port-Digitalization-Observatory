\section*{Data availability}
All data used are public: ANTAQ \emph{Estat\'istico Aquavi\'ario} (vessel
calls and operational times), Comex Stat (customs-unit trade flows with
declared freight) and the \emph{Di\'ario Oficial da Uni\~ao} (ordinances dating
the entry into production of the single window). The complete pipeline
(DuckDB build, estimation, tables, figures and numeric macros), the
intervention registry with document sources, the tests and the Observatory
application are available at
\url{https://github.com/valbergregory/Port-Digitalization-Observatory}
(\texttt{targets::tar\_make()} regenerates every number in the manuscript).
