\label{sec:infoquality}

Coverage of the documentary time decomposition is far from uniform.
In Santos, the share of vessel calls with a recorded $T_4$ rises from
\CobSantosPre\ in 2010--2011 to \CobSantosPos\ from 2012 onwards, immediately
after the single window entered production; in Rio de Janeiro it rises from
\CobRioPre\ to \CobRioPos\ in 2014. Elsewhere, coverage changes are unrelated
to treatment timing (\Cref{fig:coverage}).
