The anticipation placebo shifts each treatment date twelve months earlier
and is estimated on the true pre-period only. For cabotage $T_2$ it returns
\PlaceboCabTdoisBeta\ ($p=\PlaceboCabTdoisPwcb$): the apparent reduction in
$T_2$ predates adoption and is therefore a pre-trend, not an effect. The
$T_4$ placebo is not distinguishable from zero ($p=\PlaceboCabTquatroPwcb$).
