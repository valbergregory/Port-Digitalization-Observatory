The integration layer holds \Natracacoes\ vessel calls (\Anoinicio--\Anofim),
\Npainellinhas\ port--month observations and \Nfluxoscomercio\
customs-unit--partner--month trade flows, alongside a registry of
digitalisation ordinances with confidence grades. Every number in this
manuscript is generated from that layer.
