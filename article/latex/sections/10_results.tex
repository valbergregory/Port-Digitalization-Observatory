\label{sec:res_info}

recorded for \CobSantosPre\ of vessel calls in Santos and \CobRioPre\ in Rio
de Janeiro; after it, for \CobSantosPos\ and \CobRioPos\ respectively

\subsection{Operational times: no aggregate effect}
\label{sec:res_times}

Across dated public ports, the static effect of the single window on
documentary waiting times is \TdoisBetaFull\ for $T_2$ (wild-bootstrap
$p=\TdoisPwcbFull$) and \TquatroBetaFull\ for $T_4$ ($p=\TquatroPwcbFull$);
the physical queue ($T_1$) shows no movement (\Cref{tab:inferencia}).
